# Turbo Town — Plan & Progress

How to use this file:
- `[x]` = done, `[ ]` = not started, `[~]` = in progress / partly done.
- Add new ideas under **Backlog** in the section that fits; move them up to a
  milestone when you decide to build them.
- When something ships, tick it and add a line to the **Log** at the bottom.

Tech: Godot 4.7.2 · Forward+ renderer (Vulkan) · Jolt physics at 120 Hz with
physics interpolation · all code in GDScript · vehicle models generated by
Blender scripts in `tools/blender/` (`make_<vehicle>.py`, `make_wheels.py`).

## v0.1 — Core prototype (done)

### Driving
- [x] Raycast suspension (springs, dampers, bump stops, anti-roll bars)
- [x] Slip-based tire model with friction circle; grip varies by surface
- [x] Engine torque curve, automatic 5-speed gearbox, reverse, engine braking
- [x] Speed-sensitive steering, handbrake slides, drag and downforce
- [x] Assists: traction control, stability control, mid-air leveling, auto-hold (T = drift mode)

## v0.2 — Traffic & polish

## Known issues
- The highway/avenue junctions are at grade with traffic lights (no ramps or bridges), so
  highway traffic sometimes stops at a red light.
- Vehicle sounds were balanced by measured levels (recordings analysed offline), not by ear.
- Headless runs print a harmless "ObjectDB instance leaked" warning at exit.
- Buses and trucks swing a little into the oncoming lane on tight city corners (like real
  ones); rarely this jams an intersection until a stuck car gives up and is removed.
- Long cars scrape their bumpers in the loop (the loop's surface is frictionless for bodywork
  so it doesn't slow them down).
